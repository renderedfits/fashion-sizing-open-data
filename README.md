# Fashion sizing open data (Rendered Fits)

Four open datasets on how fashion stores present size and fit online, published by [Rendered Fits](https://www.renderedfits.com/) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Each has a DOI on Zenodo, so you can cite it directly. Mirrors are on Hugging Face and Kaggle.

| Dataset | What it covers | DOI | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Shopify Size Chart Report 2026](data/size-chart-report-2026/) | Size charts, fit notes and model information on 829 Shopify fashion stores (107,138 sized products, 24 Sep 2026) | [10.5281/zenodo.24165866](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24165866) | [renderedfits.com/size-chart-report-2026](https://www.renderedfits.com/size-chart-report-2026/) |
| [Sizing information by category](data/sizing-by-category-2026/) | Measurements, sizing advice, model size and fit notes in product descriptions, by garment category (459 stores, 74,111 sized products, 21 Oct 2026) | [10.5281/zenodo.24165871](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24165871) | [renderedfits.com/virtual-try-on-for](https://www.renderedfits.com/virtual-try-on-for/) |
| [AI Sizing Study 2026](data/ai-sizing-study-2026/) | What AI shopping assistants receive about size for one product from each of 1,383 UK and US Shopify clothing stores (anonymised, 21–22 Oct 2026) | [10.5281/zenodo.24165873](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24165873) | [renderedfits.com/ai-sizing-study-2026](https://www.renderedfits.com/ai-sizing-study-2026/) |
| [UK size 12 measurements](data/uk-size-12-measurements-2026/) | Bust, waist and hip ranges for UK women's sizes 8 to 18 across 26 retailer size charts (12 Nov 2026) | [10.5281/zenodo.24165875](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24165875) | [renderedfits.com/uk-size-12-measurements](https://www.renderedfits.com/uk-size-12-measurements/) |

## Headline findings

- Only **31.6%** of 829 Shopify fashion stores had a size chart readable as text on the page; 27.1% published it as an image and 27.7% had none.
- Only **20.3%** of sized products said how they fit, and **73.8%** of stores never gave the model's size or height.
- On the average store, **70%** of sized product descriptions gave no measurement, no sizing advice and no model size.
- **80%** of 1,383 UK and US Shopify clothing stores sent AI shopping assistants no size information for the product sampled.
- A UK women's size 12 is a bust of **92 to 97 cm**, a waist of **74 to 80 cm** and hips of **99 to 108 cm** across 26 retailer size charts (middle values 94, 76 and 102 cm).

Method and limits for each dataset are on its page and in its folder. None is a random sample of all stores; each is a dated snapshot.

## How to cite

Cite the dataset you use with its DOI, for example:

> Rendered Fits (2026). Shopify Size Chart Report 2026: size charts, fit notes and model information on 829 Shopify fashion stores. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24165866

Reuse is free under CC BY 4.0 with a link to the source page. For the UK size 12 data, the licence covers our calculated ranges and middle values; each retailer's own figures belong to that retailer.
