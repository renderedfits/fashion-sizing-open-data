## Sample and method

1,186 domains considered; 903 readable public product feeds; 829 stores with clothing sizes; 107,138 sized products. Readable-chart subset: 262 stores.

Automated scan on 24 September 2026 of public Shopify product feeds (up to 250 recent products per store), product pages and linked size guides. Raw HTML; no JavaScript execution, login or orders.
Full method: https://www.renderedfits.com/size-chart-report-2026/#method

## Limits

- Non-random sample from the Rendered Fits market research list; not a Shopify-wide estimate.
- A chart not found in raw HTML may still be available to shoppers after a click or JavaScript.
- Pattern matching can miss unusual fit-note or model-information wording.
- One-day descriptive snapshot; no measured effect on sales, physical fit or returns.
- Percentages use the stated store or product denominator; do not combine overlapping measures. Values are rounded; do not derive exact counts from them.
