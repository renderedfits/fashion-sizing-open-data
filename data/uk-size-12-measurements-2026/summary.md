# What a UK size 12 measures at 27 retailers

Source: https://www.renderedfits.com/uk-size-12-measurements/
Publisher: Rendered Fits. Size charts read 12 November 2026.

A UK women's size 12 is a bust of 92 to 97 cm, a waist of 74 to 80 cm and hips of 99 to 108 cm across the size charts of 26 UK fashion retailers. The middle values are 94, 76 and 102 cm.

Method: body measurements read from each retailer's public women's size guide; inches converted at 2.54 cm; nothing estimated. Not a random sample. Body measurements, not garment measurements.
